# strehanakljuc.si: build brief

For the development team. Generated by `handoff-plan` from the analysis in tl-product-ideas and copied here by the init PR. The product repository is public: no client contacts, budget, prices, offers or personal names.

## What to build

A new site in Slovenian about roof work: it explains, step by step, how a roof is renovated, or built turnkey on a new house, covers flat roofs and terraces, and leads the visitor to a quote request. It is built on `tl-template-website` in the repository `astragroup-website-strehanakljuc`. There is no site on the domain today.

The site is run by a construction company in the Coastal-Karst region but does not show the company's brand: no logo, link or mark of it. Only the imprint names the company, as the law requires.

Its readers:

- homeowners with an old or leaking roof, deciding between a repair and a renovation;
- people building a new house, who want one company for the whole roof;
- owners of a flat roof or a terrace, who need waterproofing or a flat-roof renovation;
- visitors comparing another roofer's offer, who want to compare the scope and the materials.

Everything leads to one goal: a quote request, sent by a short multi-step form, or by e-mail or phone. The form is sent by Trislab's shared form Worker to the company's mailbox; nothing is stored. There is no sign-in, no price list and no stored data.

## Design direction

The template is only the frame (build, checks, languages, legal pages, hosting). The look is the site's own, in its `src/` (components, layouts, pages, styles). A site that only went through init still looks like the template's sample. It must also look clearly different from the company's main site (navy and amber) and from its facade site (mineral green and ochre).

- **Feel:** "the roof over the Karst": slate and copper, calm and technical. Photos of finished roofs from above and close-ups of the layers. The process is a numbered timeline; per step one sentence and "what you get".
- **Colours** (every text and button pair passes 4.5:1):
  - slate `#263A45` for the header, the footer, headings and dark sections (white text on it 11.8:1; on the background 10.5:1);
  - copper `#A4532A` for buttons, with white text (5.5:1), and for links on the background (4.8:1);
  - patina `#7FA79B` only on slate, for large step numerals and icons (4.5:1); never on the light background (2.4:1);
  - warm stone `#F4F1EC` as the background of the sections, white for cards;
  - near black `#1D2327` for text (14.1:1) and `#5E6A70` for secondary text (4.9:1).
- **Type:** one sans-serif family, open-licence and self-hosted; large step numerals.
- **Photos:** stock photos whose licence allows commercial use without attribution: roofs from above, coverings, layers, flat roofs and terraces, in a Mediterranean or Karst setting. No photo pretends to be a job the company did, and none shows a recognisable place outside the region. AVIF at a few widths, lazy below the first screen, inside the page-weight budget.
- **Home page, top to bottom:**
  1. the hero: a roof photo, one promise, and two large buttons: *Prenova strehe* and *Nova streha na ključ*; a smaller link to *Ravne strehe*;
  2. the steps in short, as a numbered timeline;
  3. what is included, as a checklist;
  4. the roof layers: the built-in cross-section drawing;
  5. the quote request: what happens after it ("free and without obligation"), and the button *Povpraševanje*;
  6. the footer with the e-mail and phone links and the legal pages.
- **Other pages:**
  - *Prenova strehe*: when to renovate (leaks, an old or asbestos covering, no insulation), the steps from the inspection to the handover (inspection, written offer, the choice of covering and insulation, removal and the new layers, flashing and gutters, handover), what is included, a subsidy note, the call to action;
  - *Nova streha na ključ*: what "na ključ" includes (structure, underlay, battens, covering, flashing, gutters, insulation, skylights) and the steps from the plans to the handover;
  - *Ravne strehe*: waterproofing, insulation and terraces;
  - *Kritine in materiali*: clay tile, concrete tile, metal and membranes, with pros, cons and the roof pitch each needs;
  - *Pogosta vprašanja*: permit or maintenance, the season, leaks, subsidies, as an accordion that works without JavaScript;
  - *Povpraševanje*: the form, what happens next, and the e-mail and phone links with a list of what to send.
- **The site's own components:** the two-journey chooser, the numbered timeline (vertical on a phone), the "what is included" checklist, the roof-layers cross-section (one drawn SVG: structure, vapour barrier, insulation, underlay, battens, covering, with its labels as text), the coverings comparison, the FAQ accordion, the multi-step quote form, and a sticky enquiry button at the bottom on phones.
- **Navigation:** Prenova strehe, Nova streha na ključ, Ravne strehe, Kritine in materiali, Pogosta vprašanja, and *Povpraševanje* as a button. On a phone the links fold into a menu that opens without JavaScript.
- **From the reference sites:**
  - krovstvo-celje.si: the numbered steps from the form to the contract, "free, no obligation", and the permit and season questions;
  - omisli.si (its turnkey roof page): one list of what a turnkey roof includes;
  - aroundhome.de/dachdecker: the form starts with the type of work, then the place;
  - vsezastreho.net: the short "what happens next" block.
- **Design rules,** checked by `pnpm test` where a machine can:
  - a photo shows at most once on a page;
  - every photo has alternative text; a decorative photo has empty alternative text;
  - stock photos only, chosen with licences that need no credit; no photo stands for a real job;
  - text never lies on the photo itself: it sits on a solid dark panel that passes 4.5:1 and the photo fades into it; the fade is a real element, not a `::before` or `::after` (axe reports text over a gradient as "needs review", which fails pa11y in CI);
  - every link and button names where it leads, never "Read more" alone;
  - no placeholder text (sample text, lorem ipsum, TBD, TODO) reaches any page, the legal pages included;
  - every fact (the subsidy, permits) cites its source with the date it was checked; a fact that can't be checked against its source is left out;
  - no prices, no warranty, response-time or region claims, no references, no partner logos;
  - every menu link leads to a finished page in the site's design;
  - no `style` attributes in the HTML; long words in headings break at 320px.

## Domains and environments

- **Main address:** strehanakljuc.si. www.strehanakljuc.si and http redirect to https://strehanakljuc.si permanently at the Cloudflare edge.
- **Environments** on Cloudflare Pages: `dev` at dev.strehanakljuc.si (every merge to `main`) and `staging` at staging.strehanakljuc.si (release tags), both behind Cloudflare Access; `production` at production.strehanakljuc.si behind sign-in until the cutover, then at strehanakljuc.si, public. A release is promoted from `staging` after the release approver's sign-off. The steps are in the template's `docs/deployment.md`.
- **Edge:** strehanakljuc.si becomes a new zone in Trislab's Cloudflare account (tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/strehanakljuc.si/`); the domain resolves nowhere today. The zone holds the form sender's SPF and DKIM records. It is signed (DNSSEC) and blocks AI crawlers at the edge. Each environment has its Turnstile widget; the site key goes into the site's settings, the secret only to the form Worker (the template's `docs/forms.md`).
- **Search Console** (`search-console`): the domain property `sc-domain:strehanakljuc.si`, owned by Trislab's role account. Trislab creates it and adds its verification record with the key `google` in tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/strehanakljuc.si/strehanakljuc.si.yaml`, linked in `catalog/domains.yaml`; keep the record through the cutover. The sitemap is `https://strehanakljuc.si/sitemap-index.xml`: Trislab submits it after the cutover and checks the site in Search Console 4–6 weeks later.

## Integrations and outside services

- **The shared form Worker** (Trislab): receives the quote form, checks Turnstile and the fields, and e-mails the form to the company's mailbox. It stores nothing. Its address, fields and messages are set per site in tl-cloud-infrastructure; the form component is the template's (`docs/forms.md`).
- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** on the form page only; it sets no tracking cookie. The form page's own Content-Security-Policy rule allows it (the template's `docs/forms.md` section 3); every other page keeps the default policy.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics:** cookieless visitor statistics on production only (`web_analytics: true`, the template's `docs/analytics.md`); page views per journey.
- **Google Maps:** a plain link only.
- The privacy policy names Cloudflare (the challenge, the statistics), the mail provider and the mailbox the form goes to.

## Quality baseline

The template's whole baseline, no exceptions: `responsive-layout` (mobile first, no sideways scrolling at 320px), `accessibility` (WCAG 2.1 AA and a statement), `performance-budget`, `page-weight-budget`, `image-optimisation`, `caching`, `compression`, `security-headers`, `error-pages`. The form is tested with a keyboard and a screen reader, Turnstile included. The template's claims ("no JavaScript", no third-party requests) are reviewed for the form page. Uptime alerts come when the platform has them. Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page in the table exists, with a canonical address and a sitemap entry.
- `ci` is green: the build, the page-weight budget, the browser check (no sideways scrolling at 320px, axe-core), the claims check, the design check (no photo twice on a page, no placeholder text) and the `security.txt` check.
- The quote form works without JavaScript; each step's errors are shown next to the field and read by a screen reader; a test submission on production.strehanakljuc.si reaches the mailbox, and a submission without a valid Turnstile token is refused.
- The roof-layers drawing has its labels as text and an accessible name.
- The site sets no cookies; only the form page loads Turnstile, and only production loads Cloudflare Web Analytics.
- www.strehanakljuc.si and http redirect permanently (301); an unknown address answers 404.
- Google's Rich Results Test reads the `LocalBusiness` and `FAQPage` data.
- SEO (owner: the development team, unless named): the Lighthouse SEO score passes in `ci`; every page has a title, a description and one share image with alt text; dev, staging and production.strehanakljuc.si stay behind sign-in and out of search. At promotion (owner: the release approver): the go-live check on production.strehanakljuc.si. After the cutover (owner: Trislab): the sitemap is submitted in Search Console.
- The design check: the release approver compares staging with this brief's design direction (palette, type, the home page's sections in order, the site's own components); nothing looks like the template's sample, the company's main site or its facade site.
- Before production: the subsidy and permit facts are checked against their sources and dated, the legal pages are in place, and the release approver accepts staging.

## Open questions

- The subsidy note (Eko sklad co-finances roof or attic insulation, not a new covering alone) is checked against the current call before it is written.
- The search terms (streha na ključ, prenova strehe, menjava strešne kritine, ravna streha, regional roofing terms) have no measured search volume yet; Trislab checks them with a keyword tool before the cutover.
- The form Worker and its template component are built first (shared code); the site's cutover waits for them.
